The three fates

Swipe. That’s the whole interface.

There are no action buttons on a card. Right finishes it. Left sends it back to the queue. Down lets it go. Double-tap flips the card for notes.

  • →

    Swipe right · Done

    The green panel grows from the leading edge. The task leaves your three.

  • ←

    Swipe left · Defer

    The amber panel grows from the trailing edge. The task returns to the back of Up next.

  • ↓

    Swipe down · Drop

    The card washes red, and the title stays readable. Dropped has its own list.
