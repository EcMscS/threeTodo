Around your three

The rest waits. Your story stays.

Three Todo is not a general task manager. Queue, history, and notes exist to protect the three — not to grow past them.

  • Up next

    Everything beyond the three waits in the queue. When a slot opens, the top item promotes itself. No manual shuffle.

  • Your story

    History keeps what you finished, deferred, or dropped. A quiet record — not a scoreboard.

  • Dropped

    Letting go is a first-class action. Dropped tasks have their own place, so they don’t haunt the list.

  • Notes on the back

    Double-tap a card to flip it. Notes live on the reverse, with when it was added and how often you’ve deferred it.

On every screen

The same three, wherever you are.

  • iCloud sync

    Your three, your queue, and your story stay in step across iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch.

  • Apple Watch

    A redesigned Watch app for a glance at your three — and the same Done, Defer, and Drop.

  • iPhone Duo & foldables

    The layout adapts when a foldable or iPhone Duo is open, so the three still read as three.

  • Siri & Shortcuts

    Say “Add Task” or “Show my three.” The constraint holds, even when your hands are full.

  • How it works

    A short tutorial lives in the app, so the gestures are clear before you swipe the first card.
